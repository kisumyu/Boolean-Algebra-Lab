📘 Boolean Laws

IdentityA AND TRUE = A
A OR FALSE = A
DominationA AND FALSE = FALSE
A OR TRUE = TRUE
IdempotentA AND A = A
A OR A = A
ComplementA AND NOT A = FALSE
A OR NOT A = TRUE
Double NegationNOT (NOT A) = A
AbsorptionA OR (A AND B) = A
A AND (A OR B) = A
De MorganNOT (A AND B) = NOT A OR NOT B
NOT (A OR B) = NOT A AND NOT B
DistributiveA AND (B OR C)
= (A AND B) OR (A AND C)
